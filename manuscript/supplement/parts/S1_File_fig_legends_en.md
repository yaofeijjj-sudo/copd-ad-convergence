Fig A. Soft-threshold selection for the lung and brain co-expression networks.
(a, b) Scale-free fit index (signed R²) and mean connectivity at different soft-threshold powers for the lung and brain networks. The blue dashed line and red filled point mark the selected power (β = 8 for the lung and 12 for the brain); the red dotted line marks the scale-free fit reference (0.85 for the lung and 0.80 for the brain).

Fig B. Functional annotation of Axis B and tests in independent cohorts.
(a) Oxidative phosphorylation and electron transport terms for Axis B in GO, KEGG and Hallmark. The x axis gives the gene ratio, which is the number of overlapping genes divided by the 434 Axis B genes. Point size shows the number of overlapping genes, and point shade shows −log_{10} q. (b) Covariate-adjusted standardized case–control differences in the Axis B score (SD) with 95% CI in the independent lung cohort GSE47460 (0.00 SD; 95% CI −0.29 to 0.29), in the six independent frontal cortex datasets and their random-effects pooled estimate (−0.49 SD; 95% CI −0.64 to −0.34), and in the pooled estimate after adding the CIBERSORTx-estimated neuronal proportion (−0.20 SD; 95% CI −0.46 to 0.06).
